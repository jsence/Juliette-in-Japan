---

Japanese job postings often contain a large amount of *keigo* convention — set phrases welcoming
applicants and emphasising teamwork — alongside the concrete requirements. Separating the two is a
useful reading skill.

In a typical engineering posting, the requirements that carry weight are usually short:
business-level Japanese, a specific technical stack, and eligibility for a work visa. Much of the
surrounding text is tone rather than criteria. Learning to read past the politeness to the
substance is its own skill.
