I took one real engineering job posting and tried to separate the actual requirements from the
polite scaffolding. A surprising amount of the text is *keigo* convention — set phrases about
welcoming applicants and valuing teamwork — rather than concrete criteria.

The requirements that mattered were short: business-level Japanese, a specific stack, and
eligibility for a work visa. Everything else was tone. Learning to skim past the politeness to
the substance is its own skill, and one I clearly still need to practise.
