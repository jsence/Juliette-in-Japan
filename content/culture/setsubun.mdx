This week I read about **Setsubun** (節分), the day before the start of spring in the old calendar.
The custom I keep seeing is *mamemaki* — throwing roasted soybeans out the door (and at a family
member wearing an *oni* mask) while shouting *&ldquo;Oni wa soto, fuku wa uchi&rdquo;* — demons
out, fortune in.

I&apos;m not going to pretend I understand its full history. But it&apos;s a nice reminder that a
lot of &ldquo;learning Japanese&rdquo; is really learning the year it&apos;s lived through.
