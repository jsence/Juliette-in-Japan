---

**Setsubun** (節分) falls on the day before the start of spring in the traditional Japanese
calendar. Its best-known custom is *mamemaki* — throwing roasted soybeans out of the door, and
at a family member wearing an *oni* (demon) mask, while calling out
*&ldquo;Oni wa soto, fuku wa uchi&rdquo;* (&ldquo;demons out, fortune in&rdquo;).

The ritual is one example of how the Japanese year is organised around seasonal markers, many of
which carry their own customs, foods and set phrases.
