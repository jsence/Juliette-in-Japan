---

For a while the kanji felt like collecting — each new one was a small win and the deck grew fast.
Somewhere past the first hundred that changed. The readings started colliding: 生 alone has more
readings than I can hold, and I kept confusing on'yomi and kun'yomi in context.

What actually helped was dropping the count as a goal and reviewing fewer cards more carefully.
I now cap new kanji at five a day and let the words carry the readings instead of memorising them
in isolation. Slower, but it sticks.
