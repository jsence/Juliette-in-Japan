---
title: "は vs が finally stopped being a coin flip"
date: "2026-08-11"
tags: ["grammar", "particles"]
summary: "After weeks of guessing, the topic/subject distinction started to feel intuitive rather than memorised."
---

I stopped trying to translate は and が into English words. Once I framed は as *&ldquo;as for
this, here&apos;s the comment&rdquo;* and が as *&ldquo;this is the one&rdquo;*, the example
sentences in Genki suddenly agreed with my intuition more often than not.

Still not perfect — long sentences trip me up — but it&apos;s no longer a coin flip. Progress that
you can feel is rare, so I&apos;m noting it here.
