I have enough apps. What I didn&apos;t have was a place that was mine — where progress is visible,
where the data is something I can point to, and where I can&apos;t quietly skip a week.

So this site is the accountability. The readings come from KANJIDIC2, the definitions from JMdict,
and I refuse to let it generate Japanese it can&apos;t source. If it&apos;s here, I can defend it.
